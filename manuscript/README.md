# Manuscripts
This folder holds manuscripts arising from the project.
## Policy
- Only Tier 2+ material (independently reproduced or checked) goes in a manuscript; claims must state their tier.
- Suggested first manuscripts: (1) a verified survey of barriers and what they rule out; (2) a compendium of small-case computations (formula/circuit sizes) with reproducible code; (3) a magnification-gap analysis.
- One subfolder per manuscript: `manuscript/<slug>/` with `main.tex`, `refs.bib`, and a `STATUS.md` (authors, tier, open review items).
- Authorship: contributors whose merged work is used are offered authorship; AI tools are acknowledged, not listed as authors.
- Start from `template/main.tex`.
